Detail modal — compare

Isolated from index.html. Same .book3d + .detail-cover + .modal-content CSS. Toggle variants, then Open modal. Variant B needs scrolling inside the modal.

Variant A: on open the book does a quick 1.4s tilt demo (reveals spine + gloss) and the rotate-3d badge pulses. Both stop on first hover/drag. No layout change.

Variant B: cover starts at ~80% modal width (hero). As you scroll the modal content, it tilts rotateY −18°, shrinks to normal size, and then scrolls away with the page. More dramatic, pushes metadata below the fold.

About this book

From the legendary music producer, a master at helping people connect with the wellsprings of their creativity, comes a beautifully crafted book — many years in the making — that offers that same deep wisdom to all of us. Many famed music producers are known for a particular sound that has its day. Rick Rubin is known for something else: creating a space where artists of all different genres and traditions can hone themselves and become who they really are. We tend to think of the artist’s work as the output. The actual work of the artist is a way of being in the world. This book is about that way of being.
